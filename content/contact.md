## What to write about

### General questions and feedback

Questions about an article, suggestions for topics, or general comments — send them as a **General question** inquiry. We read everything, even if we cannot reply to every message individually.

### Corrections

We take technical accuracy seriously. If you spot an error — a wrong flag, a misattributed CVE, a command that does not work — please select **Correction or technical error** and include:

- The URL of the article
- The specific text or command that is wrong
- A source or citation for the correct information

We acknowledge confirmed corrections in the article with an updated `lastmod` date.

### Guest contributions

We accept guest articles from qualified security practitioners. Select **Guest contribution pitch** and include:

- A working title and one-paragraph synopsis
- A rough outline
- Your background and any prior published work
- Target word count

Review our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/) before pitching — it sets out our technical standards and what we will and will not publish.

### Security disclosure {#security-disclosure}

If you have found a security issue in **cybersecurityelite.com itself** — not in a tool we write about — please report it responsibly via the form (select **Security disclosure**) or email [contact@cybersecurityelite.com](mailto:contact@cybersecurityelite.com) directly.

We follow these handling guidelines:

1. Acknowledge receipt within **72 hours**.
2. Provide a written assessment within **14 days**.
3. Coordinate disclosure timing with you.
4. Credit you publicly (or anonymously, per your preference) when the issue is resolved.

### Press, partnerships, and business

For media inquiries, podcast or conference invitations, sponsorship proposals, or affiliate partnerships, select **Press, partnership, or business**. Include a brief description of the opportunity and your timeline.
