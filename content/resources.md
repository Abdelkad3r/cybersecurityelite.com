The curated list of resources we actually use ourselves and recommend without reservation. Updated regularly.

## Hands-On Training Platforms

- [**HackTheBox**](https://www.hackthebox.com/) — the canonical playground for offensive security
- [**HackTheBox Academy**](https://academy.hackthebox.com/) — structured modules and pathways
- [**TryHackMe**](https://tryhackme.com/) — best onboarding for beginners
- [**PortSwigger Web Security Academy**](https://portswigger.net/web-security) — free, irreplaceable for web
- [**OverTheWire**](https://overthewire.org/) — classic Linux + crypto wargames
- [**PWN.college**](https://pwn.college/) — binary exploitation, Arizona State University

## Free Learning Resources

- [**HackTricks**](https://book.hacktricks.xyz/) — the offensive cheat sheet
- [**MITRE ATT&CK**](https://attack.mitre.org/) — the adversary tactic & technique reference
- [**MITRE D3FEND**](https://d3fend.mitre.org/) — defensive technique counterpart
- [**OpenSecurityTraining2**](https://ost2.fyi/) — full courseware, free
- [**LiveOverflow YouTube**](https://www.youtube.com/c/LiveOverflow) — high-quality binary exploitation
- [**John Hammond YouTube**](https://www.youtube.com/c/JohnHammond010) — CTF walkthroughs

## Books We Recommend

### Offensive
- **The Web Application Hacker's Handbook** — Stuttard & Pinto
- **The Hacker Playbook 3** — Peter Kim
- **Red Team Field Manual (RTFM)** — Ben Clark
- **Real-World Bug Hunting** — Peter Yaworski

### Defensive / DFIR
- **The Art of Memory Forensics** — Ligh, Case, Levy, Walters
- **Practical Malware Analysis** — Sikorski & Honig
- **Blue Team Field Manual (BTFM)** — Alan White & Ben Clark
- **The Practice of Network Security Monitoring** — Richard Bejtlich

### Engineering & Foundations
- **Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach** — Kurose & Ross
- **Operating System Concepts** — Silberschatz
- **Cryptography Engineering** — Ferguson, Schneier, Kohno

## Certifications by Career Stage

### Entry
- **CompTIA Security+** — HR filter cert; cheap, broad
- **eLearnSecurity eJPT** — best entry-level offensive cert
- **Microsoft SC-200** — for SOC analysts in M365 environments

### Mid-Career Offensive
- **OSCP** — still the canonical offensive cert
- **HTB CPTS** — increasingly accepted as OSCP alternative
- **OSWE** — web exploitation specialization
- **CRTP / CRTE** — Active Directory specialization

### Mid-Career Defensive
- **GCIA / GCIH / GCFA** — SANS, gold standard, expensive
- **HTB CDSA** — defensive analyst cert
- **CCD** — Certified CyberDefender by CyberDefenders

### Strategic / Leadership
- **CISSP** — for management track and HR filters
- **CISM** — for security management
- **CCSP** — for cloud-security leadership

## CTF Platforms & Competitions

- [**CTFtime**](https://ctftime.org/) — calendar of every public CTF
- [**picoCTF**](https://picoctf.org/) — beginner-friendly, year-round
- [**Cyber Apocalypse**](https://www.hackthebox.com/events/cyber-apocalypse-ctf) — annual HTB event
- [**Google CTF**](https://capturetheflag.withgoogle.com/) — high-quality challenges, annual
- [**DEF CON CTF**](https://defcon.org/) — pinnacle competition

## Newsletters & Podcasts

## Conferences

- **DEF CON** (Las Vegas) — annual, late summer
- **Black Hat USA** — co-located with DEF CON
- **RSA Conference** — enterprise security
- **BSides** events — local, free or low-cost; check your region
- **OffensiveCon** — offensive research focus
- **NULLCON** (India), **NorthSec** (Canada), **Insomni'hack** (Switzerland)
