This Privacy Policy explains what information CyberSecurity Elite ("we", "us", "our") collects when you visit **cybersecurityelite.com**, how we use that information, how third-party advertising and analytics services operate on this site, and the rights available to you.

This policy describes the services used on the site and how to change optional privacy choices. Where consent is required, optional analytics and advertising scripts remain disabled unless the applicable consent interface records permission.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

- **Submit a contact, pitch, or newsletter form** — the name, email address, and message or other fields you enter.

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry or to send you the content you subscribed to.

### Information collected automatically

When you visit any page on this site, our servers and third-party services automatically receive and may record:

- **IP address** — used for abuse prevention and geographic aggregation; not linked to identifiable individuals.
- **User-agent string** — browser type and operating system.
- **Referrer URL** — the page you came from.
- **Request path and HTTP status** — which page was requested and whether it was served successfully.
- **Timestamps** — date and time of each request.

The hosting and CDN providers may process technical request data under their own policies. See the provider links in the third-party services section below.

## 2. Cookies and Local Storage

This site uses a small number of cookies and browser localStorage entries:

| Storage item | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Theme preference (`localStorage`) | Remembers light/dark mode setting | Persistent until cleared |
| `cse_consent_v4` (`localStorage`) | Stores analytics and advertising choices for visitors outside the EEA, UK, and Switzerland | 180 days, unless cleared sooner |
| Google CMP consent record | Stores the choices made in the European regulations message | Managed by Google's CMP; see [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics 4 measurement identifiers, created only after analytics consent | Depends on GA4 property and browser settings; see [Google Analytics cookie usage](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11397207?hl=en) |
| Microsoft Clarity identifiers | Analytics and session continuity, only after analytics consent and Clarity Consent V2 signals | Varies by provider configuration; see [Microsoft Clarity](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/clarity/setup-and-installation/consent-management) |
| Adsterra Native Banner identifiers | Advertising delivery where the banner is enabled after advertising consent | Varies by provider; see [Adsterra's privacy policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy-managed/) |

The Adsterra Social Bar is disabled. The site source contains no AdSense ad unit placements; the AdSense publisher tag is used for the European consent message. Auto Ads are controlled by the AdSense account settings. You can delete local storage and cookies through your browser settings; doing so may reset your saved preferences.

## 3. Advertising

This site uses the **Adsterra Native Banner** on pages with an advertising slot, only after a visitor outside the EEA, UK, and Switzerland enables advertising in the custom privacy preferences. The banner is disabled for EEA, UK, and Swiss visitors while its vendor and consent-signal compatibility is reviewed. The **Adsterra Social Bar is disabled**. If you revoke advertising consent after the Native Banner has loaded, the page reloads so the script is not loaded again. Outside the regulated regions, use the footer's “Privacy and cookie settings” control to change preferences.

When you consent to advertising, Adsterra and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, browser and device details, the page URL, approximate location, and ad impressions or interactions. They may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, or personalization, depending on the ad and provider settings. Their processing is described in [Adsterra's privacy policy](https://adsterra.com/privacy-policy-managed/) and [cookies policy](https://adsterra.com/cookies/). We do not control the data practices of those providers.

## 4. Analytics

This site uses **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to understand how visitors find and use our content, only after analytics consent. GA4 may process data including:

- Pages viewed, time on page, and scroll depth
- Traffic source (organic search, direct, referral, social)
- Device type, browser, and approximate country or region
- Events such as outbound link clicks

Google documents that Analytics uses the IP address at collection to derive location information and then discards it before logging. This is not described here as anonymization at collection. Data is processed under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We also use **Microsoft Clarity** for session-replay and heatmap analysis to improve page usability. Clarity loads only after analytics consent and receives separate analytics-storage and advertising-storage values through its Consent V2 API. Clarity data is subject to [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding personal information we hold about you:

### GDPR (EU / UK / EEA residents)

- **Right of access** — request a copy of your personal data.
- **Right to rectification** — request correction of inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to restrict processing** — request that we limit how we use your data.
- **Right to data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to object** — object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
- **Right to withdraw consent** — withdraw consent at any time (e.g., unsubscribe from the newsletter via the link in any email).
- **Right to lodge a complaint** — file a complaint with your national data protection authority.

### CCPA (California residents)

California residents have the right to:

- Know what personal information is collected about them and how it is used.
- Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed, and to whom.
- Opt out of the sale of personal information. **We do not sell personal information.**
- Request deletion of personal information.
- Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of the above rights, email **privacy [at] cybersecurityelite.com** with the subject line "Privacy Request" and a description of your request. We will respond within 30 days (or within the timeframe required by applicable law).
